# Contributions

SBJ AI Governance Architecture V1.0 is a frozen public baseline maintained by Solutions by Jewel.

External contributions are not accepted for V1.0.

Public repository materials may be reviewed and referenced subject to the repository's intellectual-property and use notice. Proposed architecture changes, extensions, implementation logic, scoring logic, control logic, schemas, enforcement mechanics, or methodology modifications are outside the V1.0 public contribution model.

Future contribution policies may be defined separately for later versions or public technical projects.
