# V1.0 Real-World Benchmark Summary

## Result

**VALIDATED WITH MATERIAL LIMITATIONS**

The benchmark tested whether SBJ AI Governance Architecture V1.0 could make governance determinations across publicly documented real-world AI systems and operating contexts.

The benchmark evaluates **SBJ architecture coverage**. It does not score, certify, or grade the referenced companies or executives.

## Insufficient-Evidence Determination

The sole insufficient-evidence determination concerned Replit. Public evidence did not establish a governance-grade persistent identifier for every consequential security execution/remediation chain. The limitation concerning fully automatic post-remediation adversarial retesting was preserved.

## Material Limitations

The benchmark identified limitations involving:

- continuous-authorization and invalidation-trigger semantics
- technically valid permission versus legitimate semantic-action authority
- authority provenance through branching and delegated multi-agent or multi-robot chains
- governance-grade execution identity and model/software/configuration/environment version binding
- shared-responsibility evidence allocation
- evidence completeness, integrity, retention, and adverse-evidence persistence

## Scope Gap

Autonomous irreversible material financial transactions were not adequately represented in the benchmark cohort. V1.0 therefore makes no validation claim for that authority class.

## Interpretation

No structural failure was demonstrated by the six-case cohort. The result is limited to the tested scope and does not establish universal sufficiency of the architecture.
