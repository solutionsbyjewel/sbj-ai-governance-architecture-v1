# Prototype Environment I — Agentic AI

The Agentic AI prototype pressure-tests V1.0 against systems capable of pursuing goals, using tools, invoking downstream actors, and performing consequential actions.

## Public Governance Focus

The prototype evaluates questions including:

- What identity is acting?
- What goal is being pursued?
- What capability is available?
- What authority has been granted?
- What permission is technically available?
- In what environment may the action occur?
- What action was executed?
- What was observed?
- What evidence was preserved?
- Who or what remains accountable?
- Do authorization conditions continue to hold?

## Key V1.0 Constructs Applied

**Authority Envelope** bounds permitted action conditions.

**Transition Authorization** governs movement across material capability, privilege, environment, autonomy, lifecycle, or consequence boundaries.

**Derived Authorization** requires newly scoped authority after qualifying events or transitions and prevents automatic transfer or revival of previous authority.

**Independent Downstream Authority** requires downstream executors or sub-agents to possess authority appropriate to their own consequential actions.

**Execution Instance Identity** supports attribution of consequential execution chains.

**Decision-to-Action Authority Traceability** connects authorization decisions to execution instances, execution chains, consequential effects, and evidence.
