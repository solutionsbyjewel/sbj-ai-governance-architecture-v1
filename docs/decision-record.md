# Simplified Public Decision Record

The SBJ Decision Record is a governance-decision accountability and evidence practice used to document consequential AI governance decisions.

This public version is intentionally simplified.

## Public Record Fields

1. **Agent Identity** — The governed AI system, agent, or execution identity associated with the decision or action.
2. **Business Purpose** — The approved business objective or operational purpose.
3. **Decision / Action** — The consequential governance decision or action being authorized, rejected, restricted, paused, or reassessed.
4. **Authorization** — The authority basis, approval state, and applicable conditions.
5. **Affected System** — The system, environment, workflow, service, or downstream component affected by the decision.
6. **Accountable Owner** — The designated human or organizational owner responsible for the governance decision.
7. **Evidence** — The records, observations, approvals, logs, artifacts, and other information supporting the decision.
8. **Reassessment** — The event, date, condition, transition, or evidence change that requires the decision to be reviewed again.

## Relationship to V1.0

The Decision Record supports V1.0 accountability, evidence, authorization, and reassessment practices. It does not replace the Ten Governance Dimensions, Authority Envelope, Transition Authorization, or other architecture constructs.

## Public Boundary

Production templates, evidence-sufficiency rules, implementation mechanics, scoring relationships, and internal control mappings remain proprietary Solutions by Jewel intellectual property.
